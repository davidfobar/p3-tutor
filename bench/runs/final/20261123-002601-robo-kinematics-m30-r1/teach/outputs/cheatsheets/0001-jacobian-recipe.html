Jacobian recipe (objective O2)

V = [v; ω] = J(q) · q̇. Column i is the hand's motion for a unit rate at joint i alone. Everything in base coordinates. Source: Stanford CS223A, Lecture 7. Full lesson: Lesson 1.

Steps

  1. Compute ⁰Ti for every joint.
  2. zi−1 = third column of the rotation part of ⁰Ti−1.
  3. Oi−1, On = position parts of ⁰Ti−1, ⁰Tn.
  4. Build each column by joint type; axis first in the cross product.
  5. Stack the columns into the 6 × n matrix; V = J q̇.

Checks

  • Each rotary linear column is perpendicular to the arm from joint i to the endpoint (not link i).
  • Nudge one joint by a small angle and compare the hand's move with that column times the nudge.
  • Units: m/s on top, rad/s below. Reference point change affects only the linear rows.
  • Parallel columns (arm stretched out) mean a singularity.
