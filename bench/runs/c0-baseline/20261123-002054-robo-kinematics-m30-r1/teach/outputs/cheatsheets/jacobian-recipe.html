Geometric Jacobian: V = J(q) q̇

V = (v; ω) is the endpoint's linear and angular velocity. Column i is the hand velocity from joint i moving at one unit per second, all others frozen. Lesson: 0001.

Joint iColumn
Rotary[ zi−1 × (On − Oi−1) ; zi−1 ]
Prismatic[ zi−1 ; 0 ]

Procedure

  1. From 0Ti−1: zi−1 = third column of the rotation block; Oi−1 = position column. Base frame.
  2. From 0Tn: the endpoint On.
  3. Lever arm = On − Oi−1 (to the endpoint, not along link i).
  4. Axis first in the cross product: z × r.

Checks

  • Move one joint: does the hand go where the column says?
  • Rotary linear part is perpendicular to both z and r; its length is the distance from the endpoint to the axis.
  • Top rows = ∂p/∂q (finite-difference check). Bottom rows are not derivatives of orientation angles.
  • Changing the endpoint changes only the linear rows (add ω × offset).
  • Columns parallel (det J = 0 for square J) means a singularity.
