# Robot Jacobian and Velocity Kinematics Resources

## Knowledge

- [Open textbook: "Differential Kinematics" (Clemson, Wang robotics)](https://opentextbooks.clemson.edu/wangrobotics/chapter/differential-kinematics/)
  Free chapter building the geometric Jacobian, singularities and inverse velocity. Use for: primary reading on O2 and what follows.
- [Notes: ME/AER 676 Robot Modeling & Control (Poonawala)](https://poonawalalab.github.io/rmc-s21/assets/pdf/00_RMC_Notes.pdf)
  Lecture notes based on Spong, Vidyasagar and Hutchinson; geometric vs analytical Jacobian and a planar elbow example. Use for: checking the DH-convention version of the formulas.
- [Book chapter: Spong, Hutchinson, Vidyasagar, _Robot Modeling and Control_, ch. 4 (O'Reilly)](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/robot-modeling-and/9781119523994/c04.xhtml)
  The standard textbook chapter on velocity kinematics and the Jacobian. Use for: authoritative derivation and exercises.
- [Paper: Geometric Jacobians for the Smokie manipulator and the Barrett WAM (arXiv)](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1707.04821)
  Worked geometric Jacobians for real arms. Use for: a larger example to test code against.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- Robotics Stack Exchange (robotics.stackexchange.com)
  Moderated Q&A. Use for: debugging a Jacobian whose numeric check fails.
